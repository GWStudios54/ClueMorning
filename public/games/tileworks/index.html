Rules & scoring

Words read left-to-right or top-to-bottom. The first play must cover the center star; later plays must connect to the board. Newly covered DL/TL squares multiply that tile, and DW/TW squares multiply the word. Using all seven rack tiles in one turn earns a 50-point rack bonus. The match ends when a rack empties after the bag is exhausted, or after six consecutive scoreless turns. Remaining rack values are deducted at the end.
