What is Last Call?

Last Call is Clue Morning’s daily push-your-luck trivia game. Instead of asking for one exact answer, each round gives you a category with several possible choices. Your job is to recognize which answers belong and decide how much risk to take before banking the round.

How to play Last Call

  1. Read the category and the available answers.
  2. Choose an answer you believe belongs in the category.
  3. A correct pick keeps the round alive and increases the value at risk.
  4. Bank when you are satisfied, or keep pushing for a bigger score.
  5. A bad pick can wipe out the unbanked value, so knowing when to stop matters as much as knowing the category.

Why it plays differently from normal trivia

Last Call rewards judgment as well as knowledge. A player who recognizes every answer can push aggressively, while an uncertain player can protect a smaller score by banking early. Two people can know the same facts and still finish with very different totals.

A fresh shared challenge every day

Everyone receives the same daily Last Call board, so scores are directly comparable. Last Call is the seventh game in the Clue Morning morning run alongside Letter Grid, Four Groups, Letter Trail, Triple Link, Word Steps, and Deep Cut.
