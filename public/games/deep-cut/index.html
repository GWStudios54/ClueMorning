How to play Deep Cut

  1. Read the category and think of one answer that fits.
  2. You have 25 seconds and one locked-in response for each prompt.
  3. Valid answers score from 30 to 100 points. Familiar answers score less; less-obvious answers score more.

What makes an answer rare?

Clue Morning keeps an editorial ordering of accepted answers for each closed category. That gives every player a stable score immediately, even when only a few people have played that day. The answer lists can be expanded and refined as the game grows.

A new eight-prompt set every morning

Deep Cut is part of Clue Morning’s free six-game daily set. Everyone receives the same eight prompts for the day, so scores are directly comparable.
